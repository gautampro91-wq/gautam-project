🥛 Milk Boil Alarm

A simple device that warns you when milk is about to boil.

🚨 The Problem

Milk can suddenly rise and spill when it gets close to boiling. This wastes milk, makes a mess and requires constant watching.

💡 How It Works

🥛 Hot Milk → 🌡️ Bimetal Sensor → ↗️ Small Lever → ⚡ Circuit → 🔔 Buzzer
1. Heat
Heat from the milk reaches the sensor.
2. Sensor moves
The bimetal bends or snaps when hot.
3. Lever moves
The bimetal pushes a small lever.
4. Alarm sounds
The lever closes the circuit and the buzzer turns on.

⚙️ Main Parts

🌡️ Bimetal thermostat
Detects high temperature.
↗️ Small lever
Transfers movement.
🔔 Buzzer
Makes the warning sound.
🔋 Battery
Provides power.
〰️ Wires
Connect the circuit.
▣ Heat-resistant tube/mount
Holds and protects the mechanism.
